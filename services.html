Services

What we build

Every project gets the same approach: a careful plan, honest communication, and finish work done right. Here's what ML Designs takes on across Lake and Porter counties.

The Specialty

Trim & Finish Carpentry

Trim is the difference between a house that got remodeled and a home that looks finished. It's the part of the job most contractors rush, and it's the part we're known for. Crisp reveals, tight miters, and details that hold up to a close look.

  • Interior doors, casing, and baseboard
  • Wainscoting, shiplap, and accent walls
  • Exposed beams and ceiling details
  • Built-ins, mantels, and floating shelves
  • Stair trim and railings
Full Remodels

Kitchens & Baths

The rooms you use most deserve more than a facelift. We handle kitchen and bath remodels from demo through the final fixture, keeping the project moving and the dust contained.

  • Layout changes and full gut remodels
  • Cabinet installation and custom storage
  • Tile showers and backsplashes
  • Countertops, vanities, and fixtures
  • Butcher block and custom wood tops from our shop
More Living Space

Basement Finishing

An unfinished basement is square footage you already own. We turn it into family rooms, bars, home offices, gyms, and guest suites, framed and finished to feel like part of the house instead of an afterthought.

  • Framing, drywall, and ceilings
  • Flooring, lighting, and paint
  • Wet bars and entertainment walls
  • Bedrooms, offices, and egress
  • Barn doors and custom touches
The Wood Shop

Custom Woodworking

ML Designs runs its own wood shop, so when a project calls for something you can't buy off a shelf, we build it from scratch. That in-house capability is rare for a general contractor, and it shows in the finished work.

  • Tables, benches, and furniture pieces
  • Floating shelves and mantels
  • Custom doors and door restoration
  • Butcher block tops and cutting boards
  • One-off pieces designed around your space
Outside the House

Decks & Exterior

Outdoor projects built with the same care as the finish work inside. Square, solid, code-compliant, and made to handle Indiana weather.

  • Decks and deck rebuilds
  • Pergolas and shade structures
  • Railings and stairs
  • Exterior trim and repairs
