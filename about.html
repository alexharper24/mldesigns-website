About ML Designs

Built on craft, discipline, and detail

Matt Lapina grew up around construction, and somewhere between the job sites and the sawdust he found a real love for the craft. After serving in the U.S. Army, he brought that discipline home to Northwest Indiana and founded ML Designs in 2017.

Since then the focus has stayed the same: residential remodeling with a specialty in high-end trim work, the kind of detail-driven execution that sets a finished home apart. An in-house wood shop means that when a project calls for a custom piece, it gets built from scratch instead of ordered from a catalog.

Matt builds things meant to last, with real quality behind them. That's the whole pitch.

The Wood Shop

Why the in-house shop matters

Most general contractors order their millwork and hope it fits. ML Designs builds custom pieces in its own shop, sized to your walls and matched to your home. Floating shelves, mantels, doors, tables, and butcher block tops all come out of the same shop that handles your remodel.
